The system is designed to move beyond traditional chart-based analysis by combining market data, macroeconomic information, news sentiment, volatility, cross-asset relationships, and regime detection into a single quantitative research pipeline.

Current research modules include feature engineering, regime detection, sector rotation, strategy backtesting, performance analysis, walk-forward testing, out-of-sample validation, parameter optimization, and Monte Carlo/Bootstrap analysis.

The broader objective is to investigate whether macro and market information can be transformed into statistically robust, systematic trading signals across indices and other asset classes.
